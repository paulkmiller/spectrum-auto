Services

Anything with twelve volts running through it.

Every job starts with an in-person consultation, and every install carries the same standard: deadened, sealed, properly fused, measured and tuned. Below is the range — if what you want isn't listed, ask anyway.

01

Speaker & component installs

Coax, components, three-ways. Deadening and sealed foam rings included on every one.

02

Amplifiers & oscilloscope tuning

Sized to the load, wired to spec, and every gain set on a scope before it leaves.

03

DSP tuning & measurement

Time alignment, crossover work and measured EQ using proper acoustic measurement gear.

04

Custom subwoofer enclosures

Modeled to your target response and built to fit your vehicle, not a generic box size.

05

3D-printed & bespoke fabrication

Scanned, modeled and printed parts for things nobody makes a bracket for.

06

Sound deadening

Doors, floors, trunk, roof. Road noise down, midbass up, rattles gone.

07

Remote start & security

Clean, documented installs on modern CAN-bus vehicles. No cut corners at the ignition harness.

08

Wiring repair & troubleshooting

Someone else's melted mess, a phantom draw, an aftermarket install gone wrong. This is the work I like most.

09

Head units, CarPlay & Android Auto

Retaining steering wheel controls, factory cameras and chimes — properly integrated.

10

Lighting

Interior, underglow, off-road and accent lighting, fused and switched like it came that way.

11

Dash cams & radar

Hardwired, hidden, and set up so you never think about it again.

On pricing

Labor is what you're paying for.

Every job is quoted after an in-person consultation. Labor is the majority of what you pay for, and it's the part that determines whether the equipment performs.

You can bring your own equipment. If it isn't worth installing, I'll tell you before you spend money on labor for it.
