FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. It's a one-man shop, and the consultation is a real sit-down conversation rather than a counter transaction. Call or text and we'll find a time.

Why does an install here take longer than at a big shop?

Because the panels come all the way off, the deadening and foam rings go in, the wiring is routed and fused properly, and the system gets measured and tuned before you get the keys back. Those steps are where the sound actually comes from.

Can you work on equipment I already bought?

Usually, yes. Bring it or send me a photo of what you have and I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth installing.

Can you fix an install someone else did?

That's some of my favorite work. Send photos of what you're dealing with.

What does it cost?

Labor is where the value is, so pricing depends on the vehicle and how far you want to take it. You'll get a real number after the consultation, not a range designed to get you in the door.

Do you do anything besides audio?

Yes — remote start, security, lighting, dash cams, and general 12-volt wiring diagnosis and repair.

Where are you?

1115 Applewood Drive #3 in Papillion, in the industrial park off Applewood. Look for the unit number — it's a garage bay, not a storefront.

Question that isn't here?

Call or text — you'll reach the person doing the work, not a front desk.
