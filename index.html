The standard

Four things that are included here and optional almost everywhere else.

01

Deadened and sealed as standard

Every speaker installation includes basic sound deadening and foam rings that seal the driver to the door panel. Not an upsell — it's the only way the speaker you paid for actually performs.

02

Boxes modeled, not guessed

Custom enclosures are properly designed, modeled and tuned to your taste. I'll show you the model and the response target before I cut a single sheet of MDF.

03

Every amplifier tuned with an oscilloscope

Gains set by ear or by knob position are how speakers die. I set them on a scope, at the clipping threshold, with your source material.

04

Every DSP gets a real measured tune

Time alignment, crossovers and EQ done with the best measurement equipment I can get my hands on — not a factory preset with a smiley curve.

Recent build · Cadillac

3D-printed bolt-on door pods, made from a scan of the door.

They form to the curvature of the door, aim the speaker more on-axis with the listener, and bolt on without permanently modifying the factory panel. No off-the-shelf part exists for this car — so I scanned it and made one.
