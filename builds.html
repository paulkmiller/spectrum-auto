Cadillac

3D-printed bolt-on door pods

Printed from a 3D scan of the door panel. They form to the curvature of the door, aim the speaker more on-axis with the listener, and bolt on without permanent modification to the factory panel.

Enclosure design

Modeled and tuned enclosure

Designed around the vehicle's usable volume and the customer's taste in low end, then verified against the model once it was in the car. You see the design and the target response before anything gets cut.

Measurement

Amplifier rack, scoped and set

Every gain set on the oscilloscope at the clipping threshold with real source material, then documented so the next person to touch it knows exactly what they're looking at.

Undoing an install that was done fast instead of right. Re-terminated, re-fused, re-routed and labeled. If your car has a gremlin nobody else could find, this is the work I actually enjoy.
