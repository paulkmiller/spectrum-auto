Custom fabrication

If nobody makes the part, I'll make the part.

A lot of what limits a car audio install isn't the equipment — it's that no company sells the bracket, pod, trim ring or adapter your specific vehicle needs. I run 3D printers and scan the panels I'm working with, so the answer to "can you make it fit?" is usually yes.

01

3D scanning

Scan the factory panel so parts are designed against real geometry instead of measurements and hope.

02

Modeling

Pods, adapter rings, brackets, trim pieces and mounts designed for your car and your driver.

03

Printing

Printed in-house, so a revision is a day, not a supplier lead time.

04

Finishing

Wrapped, textured or painted to sit alongside factory trim without shouting about it.

Case in point

A speaker pod that didn't exist until it was scanned.

This Cadillac needed the front stage aimed up at the listener, and no manufacturer sells a pod for that door. So the door got scanned, the pod got modeled to the panel's curvature, and it bolts on — no permanent cutting, correct on-axis aim, factory-looking fit.
