Process

It starts with sitting in your car.

Nothing here gets quoted from a photo and a guess. I want to know what bothers you about the car now, what you listen to, and where it's going — before I ever touch a panel.

01

Consultation, in person

We sit in your car. I want to hear what bothers you about it now and what you want it to sound like — and I'll go as far in-depth as you have patience for. Nothing gets quoted from a photo and a guess.

02

A plan and a real number

Equipment options at a few price points, what the labor actually involves, and where I'd spend your money first. If something you want isn't worth it, I'll tell you that too.

03

Design and fabrication

Enclosures get modeled. Brackets, pods and adapters get scanned and printed. You see the design before it's built.

04

Install, measure, tune

Panels off, deadening and foam rings in, wiring routed and fused properly. Amps set on the scope, DSP tuned with measurement gear.

05

Handoff

We listen to it together. I'll walk you through what's in the car and how it's set, and adjust the tune to your taste — that's included, not a return visit fee.

Why it takes longer

Every step I skip is a step you'd hear.

A big shop measures a good day in cars per bay. I measure it in whether the car sounds like what we agreed it would. Those two goals pull in opposite directions, and I've picked one.

So: no quick turnarounds, no canned prefabricated solutions, no large profits. If you need it done today, I'm honestly not the right shop. If you want it done right, book a consultation.
